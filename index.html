Inbox

See who fills your inbox, then deal with them in one go.

Declutter groups your recent mail by sender. Unsubscribe from a mailing list, move everything from a sender to Trash, archive it, or mark it read. It reads message headers only, never the body of an email.

Your data

It stays on your phone.

Scanning happens on the device
Photos, videos, files and contacts are read and compared locally. We run no server that receives them.
You confirm every deletion
Nothing is removed in the background. Each clean-up ends with a screen that shows exactly what will go.
Email goes to Trash, not into thin air
Bulk deletes move mail to your Trash or Deleted Items folder, so you can still recover it. Starred mail is never touched by quick clean-ups.
No Declutter account
You only sign in to the mailboxes you choose to connect. Details are in the privacy policy.
